libzstd.so.1: Zstandard, the compression library of the chunked disk tier's codec

This engine package carries an unmodified copy of libzstd.so.1 from the build machine. The engine links it when it is built with
STRATA_DISK_ZSTD (the zstd codec of --conversation-disk-chunked, src/core/conversation_codec.cpp), and some Linux systems
(containers, minimal installs) do not have it.
